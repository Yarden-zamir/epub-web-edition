# Public-domain and open sources for the web edition

Research record of 2026-11-24 (sources research agent). Nothing in the book, the site data or the release changed.

## Method and rules

- Match evidence: SIFT keypoints and a RANSAC homography between the book image (`epub-src/OEBPS/images`) and the candidate (`pd-sources/tools/match.py`). "Inliers" are matching points that agree with one geometric transform. A true match gives about 100 or more inliers. Unrelated images give fewer than 10.
- "Coverage" is the share of the candidate image that the book image covers.
- Licence: the text that the first-party record states. If I could not reach the first-party record, the row says "not checked" and names the record that I did read. I did not infer a licence from a credit line.
- Downloads are in `work/pd-sources/<name>/` (ignored by git). Each folder holds `source.json` (URL, time) and, for Commons files, `commons.json` (Commons metadata). Commons files were taken as thumbnails (1280 to 3840 px), not originals.
- Access limits met on 2026-11-24: Wikimedia upload server rate-limited this client (HTTP 429, Retry-After 600 s). loc.gov returned 403 to curl and to the fetch tool. nli.org.il record pages show a Cloudflare human check, which I did not try to get past. pikiwiki.org.il returned 403. www.scienceimage.csiro.au does not resolve. csiro.au returned 503.

## Top recommendations

1. Replace the Spencer sheet in the then-and-now overlay with Lynch's own 1849 map (NLI FL25570575, 5688 x 10096). It is the same survey at 4 times the width. 4 of the 5 fitted landmarks are on it; Kerak needs a replacement land point.
2. Add a dated "today" layer from Sentinel-2 L2A of 2026-11-20 (tiles 36RYV and 36SYA), with the credit "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026". Do not use EOX 2025 without a decision on NonCommercial-ShareAlike.
3. Add a 1945 layer from the gridded Survey of Palestine sheet 13; it fits by grid, not by landmarks.
4. Use the 1848 level from Lynch's Official Report: 1,316.7 ft = 401.3 m below the Mediterranean (p. 43), and today's -440.61 m from the book chart.
5. Swap in higher-quality originals for fig-a07-05 (Fuller, NLI 6526 px), fig-a07-06 (Irby and Mangles, British Library 2029 px), fig-a07-08 (Lynch caravan, IA 500 ppi), fig-a06-05 and fig-a06-06 (Matson, about 6000 px) and fig-a36-03 (GPO, 2052 x 3066).
6. Fix two credits: Wellcome L0057175 (record deleted; credit "Science Museum, London", CC BY 4.0 per the archived record) and the Spencer map year (survey 1848, printed 1850).

## Files

- `pd-sources/matches.tsv`: every match run (book image, candidate, inliers, coverage).
- `pd-sources/tools/`: the scripts used. `fetch.py` downloads one URL into a new folder under `work/pd-sources/`. `cget.py` does the same for a Commons file and stores its metadata. `csearch.py` and `ccat.py` search Commons. `match.py` runs the SIFT and RANSAC match. `commons.py` is a copy of the orchestrator's helper.
