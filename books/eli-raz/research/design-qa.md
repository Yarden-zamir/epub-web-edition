# Design and UX review of the web edition

Date: 2026-11-24. Reviewer: fresh-eyes design QA agent (read-only for code).

## What I reviewed

- Build: release edition from clean worktrees of origin/main, the same steps as `scripts/publish_release.py`. Pipeline `fbaeaec` ("feat: edition corrections of proven print errors"), content `b58744e` ("feat: correction note text and format"). These were newer than the live site at review time. Both repos moved after the build (pipeline `24e4b14`, content `a4bf19e`); I did not re-check those commits.
- Browser: Playwright 1.64.1, cached Chromium. Widths 360, 393, 430, 768, 1280 and 1600, light and dark. Phones (≤ 430) with touch and mobile emulation at 2× pixel ratio.
- Parts: hero, header bar and its states, printed contents and place dots, places map (markers, popups, list, far chip, wheel), all 36 chapter and 7 part openings (393 and 1280, light), photos, inline figures, captions and credits, poems, text and data tables, charts (hover), videos, footnote and glossary popovers, navigator (chapters, filter, empty state, timeline), search (empty, results, page jump, no results), download menu, figure viewer, facsimile appendix, footer, keyboard focus order.
- Checks by script: horizontal overflow, text under 13 px, tap targets under 24 px, clipped text, axe (reduced motion), long tasks, real fonts used (Chrome DevTools protocol).
- Not found in the build: correction popovers (the edition corrections render as inline notes), figure maps from `site/figure-maps/`, the then-and-now view and translations. `build_web.py` does not read those files yet, so a reader does not see them.
- Clean: axe reports no violations at 393 light, 393 dark and 1280 dark. No script errors at any width.

Screenshots are in `research/design-qa/`. File names give the part, the width and the mode. The contact sheets `open393-*.jpg`, `open1280-1.jpg` and `dark430.jpg` show many openings or parts at once. `sweep.py` and `montage.py` re-create the screenshots (`uv run sweep.py WEB_DIR OUT_DIR [393-light,…]`).

## Findings, most important first

1. **Small phones scroll sideways and zoom out.** Where: text table `#a35-t1` (chapter "המערכת האקולוגית של ים המלח"). At 360 px it is 356 px wide in a 330 px column. The layout grows to 376 px for widths 360–375, so the mobile browser zooms the whole page out and the page moves sideways. Width/mode: 360 and 375, both modes. Screenshot: `design-qa/01-hero-360-light.jpg` (the whole page is drawn smaller than the screen). Fix: wrap `.text-table` in the same horizontal scroll box that the data tables use, or let its cells wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`). Add 360 to the widths in `scripts/smoke_site.py`, because the smoke test checks only 393 and 1280.

2. **Dark mode: the places map and its popups stay bright white.** Where: `#places-map`, `.leaflet-popup-content-wrapper`, `.map-chips`. The map tiles and the white popup are the brightest things on a dark page. Width/mode: all widths, dark. Screenshots: `design-qa/popup-7-1280-dark.jpg`, `design-qa/04b-popup-430-dark.jpg`, `design-qa/dark430.jpg`. Fix: in dark mode, dim the tile pane (for example `.leaflet-tile-pane { filter: brightness(.7) contrast(1.1) saturate(.8) }`) or use a dark base layer. Give the popup, its tip and the chips `--paper` and `--ink`.

3. **The box of the withheld image is empty for sighted readers and bright in dark mode.** Where: `figure .withheld` in chapter a19 ("התריאק של ים המלח"). The explanation text is only in `aria-label`, so a sighted reader sees an empty dashed box. `book.css` hard-codes `#f1eee8`, so in dark mode it is a light rectangle. Width/mode: all widths; worst in dark. Screenshots: `design-qa/06-withheld-430-dark.jpg`, `design-qa/06-withheld-393-light.jpg`. Fix: show the text of the label inside the box (web layer). In `reader.css`, set the box to `--mat` and `--muted` so that dark mode applies.

4. **Opening the navigator and searching feel slow; scrolling has long tasks.** Where: `#toc-dialog`, `#search-dialog`, the whole page (9,734 elements, 316 facsimile pages). On a fast desktop CPU without throttling: navigator open 400–450 ms (most of it is style and layout after `showModal`), first search about 1.1 s, a 1.3 s long task during load, 50–500 ms long tasks while scrolling. A mid-range phone is about 4× slower. Width/mode: 393, light. Screenshot: none (measured). Fix: add `content-visibility: auto` with `contain-intrinsic-size` to `.doc` and `.facsimile-page`, so off-screen chapters skip style and layout. Build the facsimile appendix only when the reader opens it. Load the search index when the search button gets focus or hover.

5. **English text renders in Times, next to sans-serif Hebrew.** Where: `[lang="en"]` in `book.css` (Times New Roman), for example chapter a06 "The Life Story of the Dead Sea", its footnote popovers, its entry in the contents, and the service names in `#send-menu`. It looks like a missing font, not a choice. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/05-open-a06-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/07-footnote-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/10-send-menu-393-light.jpg`. Fix: on the web, set `[lang="en"]` to the same sans stack as the Hebrew text. The bundled Noto Sans Hebrew subset has no Latin letters, so add Noto Sans Latin or use the system sans.

6. **The interface and the book use two different Hebrew typefaces.** Where: `--sans` in `reader.css` starts with `system-ui`, so the header, buttons and dialogs use the system font (here DejaVu Sans; Roboto on Android; SF on iPhone). The body text uses the bundled Noto Sans Hebrew ("Book Sans"). The difference shows in the header, the hero buttons and the navigator rows. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/01-hero-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/08-toc-393-light.jpg`. Fix: put `"Book Sans"` first in `--sans`. The fonts are already preloaded.

7. **The hero scrolls under the transparent header and collides with its buttons.** Where: `.nav` without `[data-solid]` over `.hero`. While the hero scrolls away, the H1, the subtitle and the cover pass under "פרקים" and the search icon with no background. Width/mode: 393 and 1280, both modes. Screenshots: `design-qa/02-header-a-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/02-header-a-1280-light.jpg`. Fix: make the bar solid after the first scroll step (when `scrollY` is more than the bar height), or add a dark gradient at the top of the hero under the bar.

8. **The solid header is see-through, so headings show through it.** Where: `.nav[data-solid]` (72% paper with blur). Chapter titles and photos show as a gray ghost behind the bar title on almost every screen. Width/mode: all, both modes. Screenshots: `design-qa/02-header-b-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/open393-2.jpg`. Fix: use at least 92% opacity, or a solid `--paper`.

9. **Keyboard focus: one tab stop is invisible, and the focus ring on the hero is too faint.** Where: the second Tab press focuses `.nav-title`, which has `opacity: 0` while the bar is transparent (`design-qa/focus-2.jpg`). The focus ring is `--accent` #8f3f22 on the dark hero (about 1.5:1). On the split download button, the ring has a square corner on one side (`design-qa/focus-crop.jpg`). Width/mode: 1280, light. Fix: add `visibility: hidden` to the title while the bar is transparent, together with the opacity. Use `--on-night` for focus rings on dark backgrounds. Give the split parts an outline with an offset and a radius that matches their shape.

10. **The first view of the places map piles markers together and hides two places.** Where: `#places-map`. Markers 1–15 overlap around Ein Gedi; at 1280 they cover each other and a large part of the map is empty. Places 27 "בקעת תמנע" and 28 "ואדי רושמיה" are outside the first view, and nothing points to them (only the place marked "far" gets a chip). Width/mode: 393 and 1280. Screenshots: `design-qa/04-places-1280-light.jpg`, `design-qa/04b-popup-393-light.jpg`. Fix: add a chip (or one "all places" button) for every place outside the first view. On wide screens, start closer on the Dead Sea group, or show the Ein Gedi group as one cluster marker that opens a list.

11. **Some map popups do not describe their place.** Where: `site/places.json` descriptions, shown in `.map-popup`. 16 "מצדה": a ship drawing scratched into plaster; it does not say what Masada is. 10 "נחל דרגה": the binoculars find of a missing person; it reads harshly out of context. 7 "נחל דוד": where Gershon Nadivi was last seen. 4 "בית ספר שדה עין גדי": how Eli Raz arrived, not what the school is. The thumbnail on Masada (`fig-a34-08`, white soft-rock hills) also needs a check that it shows Masada. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/popup-16-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/popup-10-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/popup-4-393-light.jpg`. Fix: the content-data agent picks, for each place, a book sentence that describes the place itself. Where only an event sentence exists, add a short lead such as "בספר:". Keep events in the chapter links.

12. **The map base layer is busy.** Where: OpenTopoMap tiles. Red hatched areas, yellow roads and labels in three scripts compete with the 29 colored markers. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/04-places-1280-light.jpg`. Fix: use a quieter base layer, or lower saturation on `.leaflet-tile-pane` (light mode too), so that the markers lead.

13. **Part openings look broken: two type sizes for one title, and no shared axis.** Where: `section.part > h2` plus `p.display-title` (for example s01 "ים המוות -" and "האמנם?", s03 "אדם וים המלח" and "בימי קדם"). The second line is larger than the first. On desktop the title sits in the text column on the right, but the photo under it is centered. Width/mode: all; clearest at 1280 and 1600. Screenshots: `design-qa/05-open-s01-1600-light.jpg`, `design-qa/open1280-1.jpg`, `design-qa/05-open-s01-393-light.jpg`. Fix: in the web layer, give `.part .display-title` the same size as the `h2`, or join them into one heading. Align the part photo with the text column.

14. **The a31 heading holds the whole subtitle, with a star in the middle.** Where: `#a31-h`. Title, source-note star and a two-sentence question are one `h2`, so the subtitle is set at heading size and the star sits in the middle of a line. Width/mode: 393. Screenshot: `design-qa/05-open-a31-393-light.jpg`. Fix: split the question into a `.subheading` (content change, record in `CONTENT-CHANGES.md`). Keep the star at the end of the title.

15. **Letter spacing on Hebrew.** Where: `.book ol.contents .part-title` (0.08em), `.toc-part-title` (0.06em), `.hero-author` (0.08em), `.chapter-number` (0.12em), `section.credits h2`. Tracking breaks Hebrew letter shapes and reads as an English style that was copied over. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/02-header-b-393-light.jpg` (part titles "ים המוות - האמנם?"), `design-qa/01-hero-393-light.jpg`. Fix: use `letter-spacing: 0`. Use weight or color for emphasis.

16. **Search: an empty panel, jargon in results, and very long snippets.** Where: `#search-dialog`. Before typing, the panel is an empty full-height sheet. Every result line shows "מיקום 438", which a first-time reader does not understand, and it wraps to its own line at phone width. Some snippets run to eight lines, so only three results fit. A photo caption ("החילוץ מהבולען. צילום: …") ranks above the paragraph that defines the term. Width/mode: 393 and 768. Screenshots: `design-qa/09-search-empty-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/09b-search-393-light.jpg`. Fix: in the empty state, show recent searches plus two example queries and the page-jump hint ("מספר עמוד"). Remove "מיקום" from the result line (keep chapter · page). Clamp snippets to three lines. Lower the caption boost.

17. **The native clear button in inputs is blue.** Where: `#toc-filter`, `#search-input` (`::-webkit-search-cancel-button`). It is the only blue item in the interface. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/08c-toc-empty-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/09b-search-393-light.jpg`. Fix: hide the native button and draw an × in `--muted`, or tint it with `--accent`.

18. **Videos still show the printed QR code.** Where: `figure.has-video > a.qr-link`. Each player has a large QR code beside its caption. A QR code on the screen of the same phone does nothing, and four of them in a row (a10) look like print leftovers. The YouTube `hqdefault` thumbnails have black bars and low resolution. Width/mode: all. Screenshot: `design-qa/06-video-393-light.jpg`. Fix: hide the QR image when the player is present. Keep a small text link "צפייה ב-YouTube". Crop the thumbnail with `object-fit: cover` and a 16:9 box.

19. **Navigator rows: place dots jump to their own line.** Where: `.toc-places` in `#toc-chapters`. On some rows the dots sit next to the author ("בולענים ואמצעי מניעה"); on others they drop under the title ("ספנות בים המלח", "סלע אֱדום", "פונון"). Long rows show up to nine dots. Width/mode: 393, 430, 768. Screenshot: `design-qa/08-toc-393-light.jpg`. Fix: give the dots a fixed column at the end of the row (grid). Show at most four dots and "+N".

20. **The figure viewer uses half of a large screen.** Where: `dialog.figure-viewer #viewer-img`. At 1600 × 1000 the photo is about 800 px wide, with black areas around it and a small caption. Width/mode: 1280 and 1600. Screenshot: `design-qa/11-viewer-1600-light.jpg`. Fix: size the image to `max-width: 96vw; max-height: calc(100vh - caption height)`, up to its natural size.

21. **The facsimile appendix is a very long single column.** Where: `.facsimile-page` ("עמודי המקור", 316 pages). On a phone each page is 160 px wide on the right with an empty left half, so the appendix is about 88,000 px long. Blank print pages show as white boxes. Width/mode: 393. Screenshot: `design-qa/facsimile-393.jpg`. Fix: use a grid (`repeat(auto-fill, minmax(9rem, 1fr))`) and leave out the blank pages, or fold the appendix into a `details`.

22. **Map text inside images cannot be read on phones.** Where: the inline map in a10 (`#a10 figure.inline`). The legend and an English credit block inside the image are about 5 px at 393 px. The English credit also repeats the Hebrew edition note under the image. Width/mode: 393. Screenshot: `design-qa/06-figure-inline-393-light.jpg`. Fix (maps agent): move the legend into larger text, remove the English credit from the image, and add a "tap to enlarge" hint on map figures.

23. **The name repeats in the hero and the footer.** Where: `.hero-author` "אלי רז" sits on top of the H1 "אלי רז חוקר הבולענים", next to a cover that shows the same words. The footer shows `.footer-title` plus `.footer-author`, and then the title-page colophon with the title again. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/01-hero-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/06-footer-393-light.jpg`. Fix: remove the eyebrow (the title already holds the name). In the footer, show the title once.

24. **Download wording promises Kindle only.** Where: `.split-main` "הורדה לקינדל" in the hero, the footer and the bar. The file is an EPUB, and the menu offers six reader services. Readers with Kobo or Google Play Books can think that the file is not for them. Width/mode: all. Screenshot: `design-qa/10-send-menu-393-light.jpg`. Fix (needs a decision): "הורדת הספר" with "EPUB · 22.4 MB", and the reader names in the menu. If Kindle is the main audience on purpose, keep the current label.

25. **Small tap targets on phones.** Where: `.place-dots` (21 × 28), `.place-name` in the places list (24 px high), `.csv-link` (22 px), `button.term` (23 px). Width/mode: 360–430. Screenshots: `design-qa/04b-popup-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/02-header-b-393-light.jpg`. The map popup close button is exactly 24 × 24 with a small glyph. Fix: at least 24 × 24 for all of them, and 44 × 44 for the popup close button and the place dots, by padding and not by font size.

26. **Text under 13 px on phones.** Where: photo credits `.credit` (12.96 px), `.edition-credit` and `.edition-note` (12.67 px), map popup (13 px fixed), place numbers (12 px), chart axis and source text (12.48 px), `.fx-label` (12 px). Width/mode: 360–430. Screenshots: `design-qa/06-chart-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/04b-popup-393-light.jpg`. Fix: use 13–14 px as the minimum on phones. The popup can use `0.875rem`, not a fixed 13 px.

27. **Chart axis titles sit far from their axis.** Where: `.chart-axis-title` above each panel. The title is right-aligned (RTL) but the y axis is on the left, so the label is the whole chart width away from its numbers. Width/mode: all. Screenshots: `design-qa/06-chart-393-light.jpg`, `design-qa/chart-hover-1280.jpg`. Fix: align the axis title to the side of the axis (`text-align: left` in the LTR chart box), or draw the y axis on the right.

28. **The frontispiece dedication reads as a loose list.** Where: `#frontispiece p` (four lines, each a paragraph with paragraph spacing). Width/mode: 393. Screenshot: `design-qa/05-open-frontispiece-393-light.jpg`. Fix: set `margin: 0` on those lines, or mark them as verse like the poems.

29. **Small wording points in the interface.** Map chip "רחוק מכאן: פסגת לנין" is unclear; use "מחוץ למפה: פסגת לנין" (`.map-chips`, `design-qa/04b-popup-393-light.jpg`). The empty filter text "אין פרק כזה" sounds blunt; use "לא נמצאו פרקים" (`.toc-empty`, `design-qa/08c-toc-empty-393-light.jpg`). The printed contents "צרור תודות" opens a chapter whose heading is "להצלחה אבות רבים", and the navigator shows the heading. This comes from the print, but a reader can think the link is wrong. A short alias in the navigator ("צרור תודות: להצלחה אבות רבים") fixes it.

30. **Openings are not consistent in small details.** Where: chapter openings. Some opening photos have a caption and a credit (a06, a30–a33, a35), most do not. In chapter a19 the opening is a tall replacement photo on black, so the chapter title is not in the first phone screen. The opening photo of a06 is the cover photo again. Width/mode: 393. Screenshots: `design-qa/open393-1.jpg`, `design-qa/open393-3.jpg`, `design-qa/05-open-a19-393-light.jpg`. Fix: show opening-photo captions the same way everywhere (for example always under the photo, small), and cap the opening photo height on phones (`max-height: 60vh; object-fit: cover`). This is low priority, because the content comes from the print.

## What works well

- The chapter openings are calm and consistent: photo, title, short rule, author.
- The popovers for notes and terms, the timeline tab and the chart crosshair with tooltip work by touch and keyboard.
- The navigator opens on the current chapter, and the history model (Back closes overlays) is solid.
- No horizontal overflow from 393 px up; no axe violations; no script errors.
