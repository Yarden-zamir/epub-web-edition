# Eli Raz: terms for the translations

Terms for the translations of this book, in both directions.
The sections up to "Quotes that the book already gives in Hebrew" hold Eli's Hebrew forms for the Hebrew translation of "The Life Story of the Dead Sea" (unit a06). Every form there comes from Eli's own Hebrew chapters. The element ID shows where.
Use these forms. If a term is not here, use the form that the rest of the book uses.
The last section, "Term choices of the careful translations", lists each choice of en/a03, en/a10, he/a06 and site/errata-he.json (2026-11-24) with its reason.

## Sentence style

- Long, formal sentences with several clauses. Commas before relative clauses ("…, שהיה…").
- Literary and biblical register: "לא כל שכן", "אך טבעי הוא", "יצאו לו מוניטין", "הפכוהו", "איפוא/אפוא".
- Dry irony and an exclamation mark at the end of a point (a07-p008 "וזאת באמצע המאה ה-19!", a36-p039, a36-p073).
- Personal voice in the first person singular when he talks about his own work (a14-p015, a36-p009). First person plural for "we humans" (a36-p052).
- Quotes in double quotes "…"; names of documents, plans and books in single quotes '…' ('תעלת הימים', 'ספר תבואות הארץ', 'המוביל ההפוך').
- Glosses in brackets after a technical or foreign term: "אגם טרמינלי" after "חסר ניקוז חיצוני, היינו" (a35-p003); "'היפוך' (overturn)" (a07-p025).
- Latin species names stay in Latin script (Haloarcula marismortui, a35-p032).

## Citations in footnotes

- Translate the prose of a footnote. Keep the citation of an English publication exactly as printed, in Latin script.
- Footnotes that are only an English citation stay untranslated and are not in the translation file (a06-fn5, fn9, fn10, fn11, fn15, fn19–fn23, fn25).
- For a Hebrew publication, use the Hebrew title only when the book prints it: Elazari-Volcani thesis "מחקר על החיים בים המלח" (sources-p002), Raz 1993 'ספר ים המלח', רשות שמורות הטבע (sources-p040), the HaMaarag report (a35-src1).
- Otherwise keep the printed English title and write "(בעברית)" for "(Hebrew)". Do not back-translate a title. The original Hebrew title is not known.

## Quotes that the book already gives in Hebrew

- James Joyce, Ulysses, in the Hebrew translation by Yael Renan (1985, named in a35-p045): a07-p009 "ארץ עקרה. מדבר צחיח. אגם וולקאני. ים המוות: בלי דגים, חסר צמחייה, שקוע עמוק בתוך האדמה... מי רעל ערפיליים... ערי הכיכר: סדום, עמורה, אדום. כולם שמות מתים. ים־מוות בארץ מתה... פות אפורה ושקועה של העולם. עזיבות". The English text in a06 also has "Dead: an old woman's:", which Eli's Hebrew quotes leave out. The a06 translation renders it "מתה: של אישה זקנה:" in its place, so that no content drops; Renan's own words for it are not known.
- Boetius and Joye: a35-p022 "ים המלח מציג אתגרים מרתקים להבנת תהליכים ביולוגיים וגבולות החיים באחת הסביבות הקיצוניות ביותר בעולם".
- Sic transit gloria mundi: a15-p012 keeps the Latin and glosses it "(כך חולפת 'תהילת עולם')". a06-p025 keeps the Latin without the gloss: the English has no gloss, and a gloss adds text.
- Bible verses: quote the Hebrew original text (Zephaniah 2:9, Ezekiel 47:11, Leviticus 2:13), not a back-translation of the KJV. Text: Sefaria "Miqra according to the Masorah", with vowels and without cantillation marks, as in Eli's own quotes (a19-p008). Cite as (צפניה ב, ט) and drop "KJV": the Hebrew quote is not from the King James Version.
